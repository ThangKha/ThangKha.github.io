Privacy Policies

Your privacy, clearly stated

This page serves as the central privacy policy directory for all apps published by Tyler's Garage. Each app has its own dedicated policy below.

Core Privacy Principle

All Tyler's Garage apps are designed with local-first architecture. Your data stays on your device. No advertising SDKs. No data brokers. No tracking across apps or websites.

The Short Version

Our App is designed with your privacy in mind. We do not require you to create an account, we do not sell your personal data, and your tracked items and preferences are stored securely on your device and your personal iCloud. We use limited, industry-standard service providers for analytics and in-app purchases as explained below.

App Data and iCloud Sync

Your tracked items, inventory priorities, search history, and app preferences are primarily stored locally on your device. We do not operate a remote server that receives, reads, or stores your personal in-app content. To provide a seamless experience across your devices, Item Tracker for Minecraft automatically syncs your app data through Apple's CloudKit (iCloud). This backup and sync process is securely tied to your personal Apple ID under Apple’s terms. We do not have access to your iCloud data. You can manage or disable iCloud sync directly through your device's Apple ID settings.

Analytics and Diagnostics

To help us understand how the App is used, track feature usage, and fix technical crashes, we use PostHog as our analytics provider. PostHog may process basic app interactions, device models, operating system versions, language, and random session identifiers. We do not send your name, email address, exact location, or the specific contents of your tracked Minecraft items to our analytics provider. PostHog processes this non-personally identifiable data on our behalf.

Purchases

Item Tracker for Minecraft offers in-app purchases (e.g., consumable time packages for video analysis). All payments are processed entirely and securely by Apple (App Store). We never see, collect, or store your credit card information, name, or billing address. We only receive an anonymous receipt from Apple to verify your purchase and unlock the corresponding features in the App. You can manage your purchases in your Apple account settings.

What We Don’t Do

• We do not sell or rent your personal data to anyone. • We do not require you to create an Item Tracker for Minecraft account. • We do not request, track, or use your GPS/precise location. • We do not display third-party advertisements in the App.

Retention, Deletion, and Security

Your local app data remains on your device until you delete the items within the App or uninstall the App entirely. Cloud backups remain according to your personal iCloud settings. We use platform-standard security and Apple’s native frameworks to ensure your data is as safe as possible, though no electronic storage can be guaranteed completely secure.
