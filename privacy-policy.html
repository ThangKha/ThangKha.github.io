Privacy Policies

Your privacy, clearly stated

This page serves as the central privacy policy directory for all apps published by Tyler's Garage. Each app has its own dedicated policy below.

Core Privacy Principle

All Tyler's Garage apps are designed with local-first architecture. Your data stays on your device. No advertising SDKs. No data brokers. No tracking across apps or websites.

Jump to app

Information We Collect

MineTracker does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information. The app processes YouTube tutorial links locally on your device to extract item checklists and quantities. All inventory lists, custom item notes, and building progress are stored locally on your iOS device using Apple's Core Data framework.

Data Storage & Privacy

All Minecraft build checklists and inventory data stay entirely on your device. Tyler's Garage has no access to your lists, data, or usage history. No data is sent to external servers or third-party analytics services.

Third-Party Services

MineTracker uses public YouTube tutorial link parsing to extract building material lists. The app does not integrate with any third-party advertising SDKs, data brokers, or tracking networks.
